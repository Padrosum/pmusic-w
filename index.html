02THE LOCAL-FIRST PROMISE

The app should
work for you.

Not the other way around.

pmusic does one thing with intention: it plays the music you already own, right where you work. Your library is a folder, not a profile. Your listening history is a local file, not a product.

It is free software under the GPL-3.0 license. Read it, change it, share it, make it yours.
